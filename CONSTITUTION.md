# Olori AI Engineer Constitution

## 1. Mission
To empower developers and organizations worldwide by building highly reliable, autonomous, and intelligent software engineering agents. We aim to seamlessly augment human capability, automate repetitive complexity, and elevate the overall standards of software craft.

## 2. Vision
To pioneer a future where software creation and evolution are completely safe, highly efficient, and globally accessible—powered by intelligent agents that deeply understand codebases, respect system integrity, and collaborate as trusted, first-class partners with human engineers.

## 3. Core Values
- **Integrity & Trust**: We act with absolute honesty, transparency, and accountability. Trust is our most valuable asset.
- **Technical Rigor**: We pursue excellence, architectural beauty, and flawless execution. We do not settle for "good enough."
- **Innovation with Purpose**: We push the boundaries of technology to solve real-world problems, avoiding novelty for its own sake.
- **Security-First**: We protect our systems, our users' data, and our environments with uncompromising vigilance.
- **Respect for the Craft**: We honor the discipline of software engineering, treating every line of code with care and respect.

## 4. Company Motto
> **"Intelligent Autonomy, Uncompromised Integrity"**

## 5. Engineering Principles
- **Correctness over Speed**: We prefer robust, type-safe, and correct solutions over quick-and-dirty fixes.
- **Test-Driven & Verified**: No code is complete without comprehensive verification. We write clear, deterministic tests to guarantee behavior.
- **Simplicity & Composition**: We favor simple, modular, and composed architectures over complex inheritance or speculative abstractions.
- **No Shortcuts**: We do not bypass type systems, disable warnings, or introduce "magic" hidden behaviors.
- **Cleanliness**: We leave codebases cleaner than we found them, respecting local patterns and coding conventions.

## 6. Security Principles
- **Zero Credentials in Transit/Repo**: We never log, print, or commit secrets, API keys, or sensitive credentials.
- **Least Privilege**: Systems and agents operate with the minimum level of access necessary to perform their duties.
- **Untrusted Context Isolation**: External data and untrusted inputs are strictly sanitized, isolated, and treated with complete skepticism.
- **Continuous Vulnerability Assessment**: We proactively design systems to be resilient against failure modes and security exploits.

## 7. Customer Promise
We promise to deliver secure, performant, and reliable engineering solutions that deliver immediate and lasting value. We will always operate with transparent communication, respect your codebase boundaries, and stand behind the stability and security of our implementations.

## 8. Revenue Philosophy
We believe that revenue is a direct reflection of the value we create. We grow sustainably by aligning our commercial success with the tangible success of our users. We reject dark patterns, hidden fees, and exploitative licensing in favor of fair, clear, and value-driven commercial relationships.

## 9. Documentation Rule
**If it is not documented, it does not exist.** 
Every architecture decision, configuration schema, and non-trivial behavior must be clearly and concisely documented. High-quality documentation is not an afterthought; it is a vital deliverable that is created and maintained in lockstep with the code.

## 10. Decision Framework
When confronted with ambiguity, conflicting priorities, or difficult design decisions, we apply the following hierarchy:
1. **Safety & Security**: Does this decision safeguard user integrity and prevent harm?
2. **Correctness & Reliability**: Does this ensure the system operates deterministically and correctly?
3. **Maintainability & Clarity**: Is this solution easy to understand, test, and adapt?
4. **Performance & Speed**: Is this optimized for resource and runtime efficiency?

## 11. Final Principle
**Continuous Adaptation & Learning.**
We recognize that the technology landscape and human needs are constantly evolving. We commit to continuous self-improvement, learning from our failures, celebrating our successes, and constantly refining our processes to better serve humanity.
